## About it

These cookies are an easy, affordable snack made with oats, peanut butter, and dark chocolate that satisfies sweet cravings while providing protein and fiber. They have a soft, chewy texture with a rich chocolate flavour and a hint of peanut butter. Store them in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days. Next time, I'd add chopped nuts or extra dark chocolate for more crunch and flavour.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup oat flour
- 1 cup rolled oats
- 2 tbsp peanut butter
- 1 egg
- 1 ripe banana, mashed
- 2 tbsp dark chocolate chips or chopped dark chocolate
- ½ tsp baking powder (optional)
- 1 scoop protein powder (optional, for extra protein)

## Steps

1. Preheat the oven
2. Preheat your oven to 180°C and line a baking tray with baking paper.
3. Prepare the wet ingredients
4. In a mixing bowl, mash the ripe banana with a fork until smooth. Add the egg and peanut butter, then mix until everything is well combined.
5. Add the dry ingredients
6. Add the oat flour, rolled oats and baking powder. If you're using protein powder, add it at this stage. Mix until a thick cookie dough forms. If the mixture is too dry, add milk one tablespoon at a time.
7. Add the chocolate
8. Fold the dark chocolate pieces into the dough, distributing them evenly.
9. Shape the cookies
10. Scoop small portions of dough onto the prepared baking tray. Shape them into cookies and flatten them gently with your fingers or the back of a spoon, as they won't spread much during baking.
11. Bake
12. Bake for 12–15 minutes, or until the cookies are set and lightly golden around the edges. Avoid overbaking if you prefer a softer texture.
13. Cool and serve
14. Remove the tray from the oven and let the cookies cool for 5–10 minutes before transferring them to a rack. They'll become firmer as they cool.

## Tips

You can swap peanut butter for almond butter or use chopped nuts instead of chocolate chips. Store the cookies in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days, or freeze them for longer storage. Enjoy them cold or warm them in the microwave for 10–15 seconds. To make them cheaper, use homemade oat flour by blending regular oats, skip the protein powder and choose a basic peanut butter.
