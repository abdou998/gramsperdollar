## About it

An easy, quick, make-ahead breakfast for busy mornings: chia seeds provide fibre and healthy fats, while yogurt and milk add protein. To make it your own, switch up fruits and toppings with what you have at home, or add protein powder for an extra protein boost.

## Ingredients

- for the pudding
- 2 tablespoons chia seeds
- cup of milk (120 ml )
- Greek yogurt ( 120g )
- 1 teaspoon honey, optional
- For the toppings
- 1 banana, sliced
- a small handful of blueberries
- a small handful of blackberries
- 1 tablespoon pumpkin seeds
- 1 tablespoon granola

## Steps

1. Mix the base; combine the chia seeds, milk, yogurt, and optional honey in a bowl or jar. Stir thoroughly to prevent clumps.
2. Refrigerate overnight. Cover and refrigerate for at least 6 hours, preferably overnight, until the chia seeds absorb the liquid and the mixture thickens.
3. Add the toppings. In the morning, stir the pudding and add sliced banana, pumpkin seeds, blueberries, blackberries, and granola. Add a splash of milk if you prefer a thinner consistency, then enjoy.

## Tips

Keep covered in the refrigerator at 4 degrees C or below. Prepare the pudding base up to 3 days ahead; also add banana and granola just before serving for the freshest taste and best texture
