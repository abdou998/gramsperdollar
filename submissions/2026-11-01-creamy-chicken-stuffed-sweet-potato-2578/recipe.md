## About it

I made this recipe for a simple, affordable, protein-rich meal. The sweet potato is soft and naturally sweet, while the chicken, garlic, onion, and melted cheese create a savoury, creamy filling. Next time, I'd try lean ground beef instead of chicken or add mushrooms for extra flavour. Store in the fridge for up to 3 days and reheat in the microwave or oven.

## Ingredients

- 2 medium sweet potatoes
- 200g cooked chicken breast, diced or shredded
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 50g cheese ( your choice)
- 2 tbsp milk, if needed for a creamier texture
- 1 tsp olive oil
- salt and black pepper
- paprika and herbs
- fresh chives or parsley for topping

## Steps

1. Preheat the oven to 200 C. Wash the sweet potatoes, add black pepper, salt, and olive oil, pierce them with a fork, and bake for around 50 minutes, until soft
2. Meanwhile, heat olive oil in a pan. Add the onion and garlic and cook until soft and fragrant
3. Add the cooked chicken, season with salt, pepper, and paprika, and mix well.
4. Add the cheese and a little milk if needed. Stir until the cheese melts and creates a creamy chicken filling.
5. Once the sweet potatoes are ready, cut them in half and gently open the middle.
6. Spoon the creamy chicken mixture over the sweet potatoes and finish with fresh herbs

## Tips

Keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat in the microwave or oven at 180 C, adding a splash of milk if needed. To save money, use cheaper chicken cuts, add vegetables to stretch the filling, and reduce the amount of cheese
