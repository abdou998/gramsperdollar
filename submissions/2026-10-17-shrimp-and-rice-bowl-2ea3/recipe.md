## About it

A simple, protein-rich meal that's both nourishing and flavorful. A savoury garlic, tender shrimp, fluffy rice. You can refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 2 days for best quality

## Ingredients

- 1 cup of rice ( about 180 g)
- 200g peeled shrimp
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon dried herbs or mixed seasoning
- half a lemon ( optional )

## Steps

1. Cook the rice separately; rinse the rice and cook it in a pot of salted water. Once tender, drain if necessary and set aside
2. Prepare the shrimp; heat olive oil in a pan. Add the onion and garlic and sauté until fragrant. Add the shrimp, paprika, black pepper, salt and herbs; cook until the shrimp are pink and opaque
3. Combine the rice and shrimp. Add the cooked rice directly to the shrimp pan. Mix gently so the rice absorbs the seasoning and the flavours are evenly distributed cook together for another 2 to 3 minutes
4. Garnish and serve; transfer to a serving bowl and garnish with fresh parsley or coriander. Add a squeeze of lemon if desired, and serve warm
