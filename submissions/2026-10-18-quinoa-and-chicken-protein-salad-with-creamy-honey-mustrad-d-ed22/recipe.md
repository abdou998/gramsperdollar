## About it

This salad is my take on making healthy eating simple, balanced, and enjoyable. With protein-rich chicken, eggs, and quinoa, fresh vegetables, healthy fats, and a homemade yogurt dressing, it's a nutritious meal without unnecessary complexity. 
Next time, I'd experiment with whatever I have in the fridge. The beauty of this recipe is its flexibility: simple, everyday ingredients can easily become a nutritious and satisfying meal.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup cooked quinoa
- 200g chicken breast
- 2 cups lettuce, chopped
- 1 cup fresh spinach
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 1 medium tomato, chopped
- 2 eggs
- 1 and a half cucumbers, sliced
- 40g feta cheese, crumbled
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- salt and black pepper to taste
- For the dressing
- 3 tablespoons plain yogurt
- 1 teaspoon mustard
- 1 small garlic clove, minced
- 1 teaspoon honey
- 2 teaspoons fresh lemon juice
- salt and black pepper to taste

## Steps

1. Cook the quinoa; rinse the quinoa and cook according to the package instructions. Set aside and allow it to cool slightly
2. Cook the chicken and eggs; season the chicken breast with salt and black pepper. Cook in a pan with olive oil until fully cooked on each side, then slice. Boil the eggs, let them cool, and cut them into halves or quarters.
3. Prepare the vegetables. Wash and chop the lettuce, spinach, tomato, and cucumber. Slice the avocado and arrange all the ingredients in a large bowl.
4. Make the dressing: in a small bowl, combine the yogurt, mustard, minced garlic, honey and lemon juice. Add salt and black pepper to taste, then mix until smooth and creamy.
5. Assemble and serve: add the quinoa and vegetables to a bowl. Top with the sliced chicken, eggs, avocado, and crumbled feta cheese. Drizzle with the homemade dressing and toss gently, or serve the dressing on the side

## Tips

You can store the salad ingredients and dressing separately in airtight containers in the refrigerator, and for the best texture, add the avocado and dressing just before eating. Use cooked chicken within 3 to 4 days when refrigerated promptly at 4 degrees Celsius or below.
