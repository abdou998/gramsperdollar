## About it

I made this plate as a simple, affordable, and protein-rich meal using everyday ingredients. The chicken is tender and flavourful, marinated in tangy mustard and lemon, while the seasoned rice and crispy potatoes make it filling and satisfying. It keeps well in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days reheat thoroughly before serving. Next time, I'd add more colourful vegetables or a fresh salad on the side for extra freshness.

## Ingredients

- For the chicken brochettes
- Chicken breast, cut into medium-sized cubes
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- Fresh parsley or coriander, finely chopped
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp paprika
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- Juice of half a lemon
- 1 tbsp mustard
- 2 tbsp plain yogurt (optional)
- Red and green bell peppers, cut into squares
- Wooden or metal skewers
- For the rice
- 1 cup rice
- ½ onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, black pepper, paprika, and other preferred seasoning
- For the potatoes
- Potatoes, cut into wedges or cubes
- Olive oil
- Paprika, salt, black pepper and your preferred spices

## Steps

1. Prepare the potatoes
2. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
3. Wash and cut the potatoes into evenly sized wedges or cubes.
4. Toss them with olive oil, paprika, salt, and black pepper.
5. Spread them in a single layer on a baking tray, leaving space between the pieces so they roast rather than steam.
6. Bake for approximately 30–40 minutes, turning halfway through, until golden and crispy. Cooking time depends on their size.
7. Marinate the chicken
8. Cut the chicken breast into medium-sized cubes.
9. In a bowl, combine the chopped onion, minced garlic, parsley or coriander, cumin, paprika, salt, black pepper, lemon juice and mustard.
10. Add the yogurt if using it, then mix everything together.
11. Add the chicken cubes and coat them thoroughly with the marinade.
12. Cover and refrigerate for 30 minutes or longer to let the flavours develop.
13. Thread the chicken onto skewers, alternating with pieces of red and green pepper.
14. Cook the chicken brochettes
15. Place the skewers on a lightly oiled baking tray.
16. Bake at 200°C, turning halfway through, until the chicken is fully cooked.
17. Check the thickest pieces reach an internal temperature of 74°C. Cooking time will depend on the size of the cubes.
18. Prepare the rice separately
19. Rinse the rice under cold water until the water becomes clearer.
20. Add the rice to a pot of boiling salted water and cook according to the package instructions until tender.
21. Drain the rice well and set it aside.
22. In a separate pan, heat the olive oil over medium heat.
23. Add the chopped onion and cook for a few minutes until soft.
24. Add the minced garlic and stir for about 30 seconds.
25. Season with paprika, black pepper, salt and any other spices you prefer.
26. Add the cooked rice to the pan and gently mix it with the onion, garlic, and seasoning.
27. Let everything cook together for another 2–3 minutes, stirring gently so the rice absorbs the flavours without becoming mushy.
28. Serve
29. Arrange the seasoned rice on a plate, add the oven-baked chicken brochettes, and serve with the crispy potatoes. Enjoy while warm!

## Tips

To keep this meal high in protein and affordable, use chicken breast or boneless chicken thighs as the main protein source. Buying chicken in larger packs and using frozen vegetables can help reduce costs without compromising the meal. You can also add a boiled egg or serve it with Greek yogurt for extra protein. Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days and reheat thoroughly, adding a little water to the rice to keep it moist.
