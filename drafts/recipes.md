## Lemon-rosemary chicken with grilled squash and crunchy veg

**Makes 2 containers · ~49 g protein each · about $3.70 a serving (Walmart, Oct 2026)**

Lemon, garlic and rosemary is about as simple as a marinade gets, and it holds up in the
fridge better than most. The chicken stays juicy on day three as long as you don't overcook
it in the first place, so get a thermometer if you don't own one. They're cheap.

**Chicken**
- 2 chicken breasts (about 400 g / 14 oz), sliced in half horizontally so they cook evenly
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lemon, zest and juice
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh rosemary, chopped fine (or 1 tsp dried)
- 1 tsp kosher salt, ½ tsp black pepper

**Veg**
- 1 yellow squash, cut into ½-inch rounds, plus 1 tbsp olive oil, salt and pepper
- 1 cup green beans, trimmed
- 2 carrots, cut into sticks
- ½ red and ½ yellow bell pepper, sliced

**Method**
1. Mix the oil, lemon, garlic, rosemary, salt and pepper in a bowl. Add the chicken, turn to
   coat and leave it for 15–20 minutes while you sort the veg. Up to 2 hours in the fridge is fine.
2. Steam the green beans and carrots for 3–4 minutes, just until they lose their raw crunch.
   Run them under cold water so they stop cooking — otherwise they go soft by Wednesday.
3. Get a grill pan or heavy skillet properly hot. Cook the chicken 4–5 minutes, flip, then
   3–4 more, until the thickest part hits 74°C / 165°F. Let it rest 5 minutes before slicing.
4. Toss the squash in the oil, salt and pepper and sear 2–3 minutes a side in the same pan.
5. Split everything between 2 containers. Keep the pepper slices in a separate small box or
   bag so they stay crisp.

**Storing:** 3–4 days in the fridge. Reheat the chicken and squash; eat the beans, carrots and
peppers cold or warm, either works.

**Cheaper:** use 1½ tbsp oil total instead of 3 (saves ~90 kcal a serving, same protein) and
dried rosemary instead of a fresh pack.

## One-pot spiced chicken, red lentil and rice

**Makes 4 containers · ~50 g protein each · about $1.90 a serving (Walmart, Oct 2026)**

This is the cheap one. Red lentils cook in about the same time as white rice, so they go in
the same pot and quietly add a lot of protein for very little money. Cumin and smoked paprika
do most of the flavour work. Per dollar, it gives roughly twice the protein of the grilled
chicken recipe.

**Ingredients**
- 600 g (1⅓ lb) chicken breast, cut into bite-size pieces (frozen breasts, thawed, are fine)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp kosher salt, plus black pepper
- 1 cup (200 g) long-grain white rice, rinsed
- ¾ cup (150 g) red lentils, rinsed
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes, with the juice
- 3½ cups water
- 1 cup frozen peas

**Method**
1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium-high. Brown the chicken for about 5 minutes —
   it doesn't need to be cooked through yet. Season it with half the salt.
2. Turn the heat to medium. Add the onion and cook 4–5 minutes until soft, then the garlic,
   cumin and paprika for one more minute.
3. Stir in the rice, lentils, tomatoes, water and the rest of the salt. Bring it to a boil,
   then cover, turn it to low and simmer 18–20 minutes. Give it one stir halfway — lentils
   like to catch on the bottom.
4. Tip in the peas, cover again and leave it off the heat for 5 minutes. Taste for salt.
5. Divide between 4 containers.

**Storing:** 3–4 days in the fridge. It thickens as it sits — add a splash of water before
reheating.
